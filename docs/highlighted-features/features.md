# Highlighted Features

A curated list of features for the Chat Bubbles app store listing and landing page, ordered from most important to least important.

---

### Multi-Channel Floating Chat Widget

- **One-line summary:** Connect with visitors across 19 popular messaging apps and direct contact channels from one floating button.
- **Description:** You can turn casual site visitors into paying customers by letting them reach you on their favorite messaging apps, including WhatsApp, Messenger, Telegram, Instagram, and email. Every channel connects directly to your verified profiles without requiring visitors to fill out slow contact forms. This direct connection speeds up response times and helps you close sales while interest is high.
- **Plan:** Free Trial, Monthly, Yearly, Lifetime
- **Where to find it in the app:** Open the **Builder** tab, click **Channels**, and toggle on your preferred platforms under **Messaging Channels**.

---

### Interactive Support Team Roster

- **One-line summary:** Introduce your real team members with custom avatars, department titles, and personal direct chat links.
- **Description:** You can build instant trust with your site visitors by presenting a welcoming team roster inside a live chat modal. Each team member can feature a custom profile photo, job title, and dedicated direct contact handle or phone number. Visitors can choose the right specialist for their question, which creates a personal shopping experience that increases conversions.
- **Plan:** Free Trial, Monthly, Yearly, Lifetime
- **Where to find it in the app:** Open the **Builder** tab, click **Support Team**, and manage your agents under **Support Team / Agents**.

---

### Smart Behavioral Triggers

- **One-line summary:** Catch visitors at the perfect moment using scroll depth detection and desktop exit-intent triggers.
- **Description:** You can present your chat prompt when visitors are most likely to respond rather than interrupting them immediately upon page load. The widget can wait until a visitor scrolls past a specific percentage of your page, or pop open the chat window right as their mouse cursor moves toward the browser tab bar to exit. These smart timing rules capture leaving visitors and recover lost inquiries automatically.
- **Plan:** Free Trial, Monthly, Yearly, Lifetime
- **Where to find it in the app:** Open the **Settings** tab and configure options under **Behavioral Triggers & Smart Display**.

---

### Business Operating Hours and Schedule

- **One-line summary:** Set your active office hours and automatically display offline notices or hide the widget outside work times.
- **Description:** You can set realistic expectations for your visitors by matching your chat widget visibility to your team schedule. When your team is off duty, the widget can automatically display an offline status notice with the time you return, or hide itself completely until your next working day. This automated schedule prevents missed expectations while keeping your customer communication honest and reliable.
- **Plan:** Free Trial, Monthly, Yearly, Lifetime
- **Where to find it in the app:** Open the **Settings** tab and turn on the schedule under **Operating Hours & Days Schedule**.

---

### Real-Time Conversion Analytics Dashboard

- **One-line summary:** Track views, channel clicks, and overall conversion rates to see which messaging apps deliver the best results.
- **Description:** You can understand visitor behavior and measure your marketing return on investment with built-in interaction tracking. The dashboard shows daily impression counts, total clicks across all channels, and an interactive volume chart broken down by 7 days, 30 days, or 6 months. A channel performance breakdown reveals exactly which platforms your audience prefers so you can focus your attention on your most profitable channels.
- **Plan:** Free Trial, Monthly, Yearly, Lifetime
- **Where to find it in the app:** Click the **Analytics** tab in the top navigation bar to open the **Widget Analytics** dashboard.

---

### Flexible Launcher and Menu Layouts

- **One-line summary:** Choose between a full Live Chat Modal or direct Icon Buttons with custom shapes, icons, and animations.
- **Description:** You can match your chat launcher to your brand style using circle, rounded square, or pill button shapes with entrance animations like pop, bounce, and pulse. Choose the **Live Chat Modal** mode for an interactive team window, or select the **Icon Buttons** mode to arrange your channels in a clean vertical stack, two-column grid, or horizontal row. Every element adapts to your page layout for a cohesive look.
- **Plan:** Free Trial, Monthly, Yearly, Lifetime
- **Where to find it in the app:** Choose your mode on the **Design** tab under **Widget Experience Mode**, then adjust button geometry under **Floating Launcher Design**.

### Mobile-Optimized Clean Display

- **One-line summary:** Keep mobile screens clutter-free with custom corner offsets and an automatic four-channel expansion cap.
- **Description:** You can protect your mobile user experience from crowded buttons that block product details or checkout controls. The mobile layout constraint caps visible buttons to your top four channels and tucks additional channels behind a clean expandable button. Independent mobile corner offset controls let you position the launcher cleanly above native mobile navigation bars.
- **Plan:** Free Trial, Monthly, Yearly, Lifetime
- **Where to find it in the app:** Open the **Design** tab and adjust settings under **Mobile Layout Constraint (Cap 4+)** and **Mobile Offset X / Y**.

- **Description:** You can blend the chat modal into your website color scheme using preset themes inspired by WhatsApp, Messenger, Telegram, and Instagram, or design your own custom multi-color gradient. Custom color pickers give you precise control over button shades, launcher accents, and label backgrounds so your chat experience feels native to your site.
- **Plan:** Free Trial, Monthly, Yearly, Lifetime

### Pre-filled Dynamic Messages and Inquiries

- **One-line summary:** Help visitors start conversations faster with pre-filled greeting text and automatic page title variables.
- **Description:** You can remove friction for potential buyers by greeting them with ready-to-send messages when they tap WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS, or email. Dynamic placeholder tags automatically pull in the current page title and web address so you know exactly which product or article the visitor was looking at when they reached out.
- **Plan:** Free Trial, Monthly, Yearly, Lifetime
- **Where to find it in the app:** Open the **Builder** tab, click **Channels**, and enter your message in the **Pre-filled Welcome Message** field.

### Floating Greeting Bubbles with Notification Chimes

- **One-line summary:** Catch visitor attention with subtle speech bubbles, unread notification badges, and gentle audio chimes.
- **Description:** You can invite visitors to chat without opening the entire modal by displaying a floating speech bubble after a brief browsing delay. An unread badge counter signals an active conversation, while an optional audio chime plays a gentle prompt when the bubble appears. Visitors can easily close the greeting bubble or click it to view your team.
- **Plan:** Free Trial, Monthly, Yearly, Lifetime
- **Where to find it in the app:** Open the **Design** tab for bubble text, and open the **Settings** tab to adjust delays and the **Greeting Bubble Sound** toggle.

### Advanced Page and URL Path Targeting

- **One-line summary:** Control exactly where your chat widget appears using page dropdowns or custom URL wildcard rules.
- **Description:** You can deliver targeted customer service by displaying specific chat buttons on high-intent pages such as pricing and checkout, while hiding them on distraction-free landing pages. Flexible path filtering supports both inclusion and exclusion lists with wildcard matching so you can manage entire sections of your site with simple rules.
- **Plan:** Free Trial, Monthly, Yearly, Lifetime
- **Where to find it in the app:** Open the **Settings** tab and configure your display rules under **Page / URL Targeting Filter**.

### Native Framer Canvas Integration

- **One-line summary:** Insert a production-ready component onto your Framer canvas and publish with zero coding required.
- **Description:** You can design your chat experience inside the plugin interface, click one button to generate a clean Framer component, and position it directly on your project canvas. The component updates whenever you modify settings in the plugin, runs at 60 frames per second on published sites, and produces zero third-party script bloat.
- **Plan:** Free Trial, Monthly, Yearly, Lifetime
- **Where to find it in the app:** Click the **Insert into Canvas** or **Update Component** button located in the bottom action bar.
