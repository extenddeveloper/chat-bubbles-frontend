# How to manage plans and billing

This guide explains the available subscription options for Chat Bubbles, how to start your free trial, how to upgrade securely using Stripe, and how to manage or cancel your site license at any time.

## Before you start

Create a free Framefic account or log in through the plugin shell so your subscription attaches securely to your account and your active Framer site ID.

## Step 1: Understand the available plans

Chat Bubbles offers flexible options to accommodate individual site creators, growing agencies, and established online brands:

- **Free Trial:** Enjoy complete access to every PRO feature for seven days on your Framer site without entering credit card details upfront.
- **Pro Monthly:** $4.99 billed monthly. Ideal if you want flexible month-to-month billing with the freedom to pause or cancel whenever you wish.
- **Pro Yearly:** $49.99 billed annually. A cost-effective option that saves you over 16% compared to monthly billing while providing uninterrupted service all year long.
- **Lifetime Deal:** $99.99 billed as a single one-time payment. Grants perpetual access to all current and future features on your site with zero recurring renewal charges.

[Add Pricing Plans Comparison Screenshot]

Every paid plan includes:

- Access to all 19 messaging channels.
- Unlimited support team agents with custom avatars and direct contact numbers.
- Custom header gradients, background palettes, and button styles.
- Behavioral triggers, including scroll depth and desktop exit-intent.
- Operating hours schedules and offline status notices.
- Real-time conversion analytics and daily impression tracking.
- Zero Framefic watermarks on your published site.

## Step 2: Start your free trial or upgrade to PRO

To begin your subscription:

1. Click the **PRO** badge in the top right corner of the plugin header, or click your profile icon and select **Subscription**.
2. If you are a new user, you will be prompted to create an account with your email and password. Upon verification, your seven-day free trial activates immediately.
3. If you are ready to upgrade, click the **Upgrade to PRO** button to view the pricing plans.

[Add Upgrade Modal Screenshot]

## Step 3: Complete checkout securely through Stripe

Select your preferred plan card (**Monthly**, **Yearly**, or **Lifetime**) and click **Continue to Checkout**.

[Add Stripe Payment Window Screenshot]

The secure Stripe payment modal will appear:

1. Enter your credit card details, billing address, and account email.
2. Confirm your purchase by clicking **Subscribe** or **Pay Now**.
3. Once Stripe processes the transaction, a green confirmation screen will confirm that your site license is active.
4. Your canvas component will sync immediately, unlocking all PRO styling and behavior controls without requiring you to re-insert the widget.

## Step 4: Manage payment methods and view receipts

You can manage your billing details directly from within the plugin at any time:

1. Click your profile avatar in the top right corner of the plugin window.
2. Click the **Subscription** button to view your current plan, renewal date, and license status.
3. Click **Manage Billing in Stripe** to open the Stripe customer portal in a new browser tab.
4. In the portal, you can update your credit card on file, change your billing address, and download official PDF tax invoices.

[Add Subscription Management Page Screenshot]

## Step 5: Switch plans or cancel your subscription

If you wish to change your billing frequency or cancel your recurring plan:

- **Switching between Monthly and Yearly:** In the **Subscription** section, click **Change Plan**, choose your new plan, and confirm the adjustment. Stripe will automatically calculate any prorated balance for you.
- **Cancelling your plan:** Click **Cancel Subscription** at the bottom of the subscription page. A brief cancellation questionnaire will appear to collect feedback. Follow the prompts to confirm your cancellation.

[Add Cancellation Confirmation Screen Screenshot]

When you cancel, your PRO features will remain active until the end of your current paid billing period, and no further renewal charges will be made.

## Check that it works

To verify that your license is active:

1. Check the top header of the plugin window. The **PRO** badge should display in vibrant blue with an active status.
2. Open the **Settings** tab and confirm that the **Behavioral Triggers & Smart Display** cards are fully unlocked and editable.
3. Open your live website to confirm that all custom themes, greeting bubbles, and channel links function without restriction.

## Troubleshooting

- **Your card payment was declined:** Ensure your billing address matches the address on file with your bank, and confirm that your card supports international online transactions.
- **Your subscription is active but the canvas shows a locked status:** Click your profile icon in the plugin, click **Refresh License**, and then click **Update Component** in the bottom bar to resynchronize your canvas instance.
- **You moved your component to a new Framer project:** Each license attaches to one unique Framer site ID. If you migrate your site, reach out to Framefic support with your receipt to transfer your license to the new site.
