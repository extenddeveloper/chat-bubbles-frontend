# How to track conversion analytics

This guide explains how to monitor visitor engagement, track message clicks across each communication channel, and measure your click-through conversion rate with the built-in analytics dashboard.

## Before you start

Make sure you have inserted your chat component onto your Framer canvas and published your site, because the analytics dashboard records real interaction data generated by visitors on your live website.

[Add Tracking Conversion Analytics Video Tutorial]

## Step 1: Confirm analytics is enabled

Click the **Settings** tab in the top navigation bar and look at the section titled **Enable Analytics**.

[Add Enable Analytics Toggle Screenshot]

Confirm that the toggle switch is turned on. When enabled, a shortcut button labeled **View Analytics Dashboard** appears in the section, and the **Analytics** tab becomes accessible in the primary navigation bar.

## Step 2: Open the Widget Analytics dashboard

Click the **Analytics** tab in the top navigation bar.

[Add Analytics Dashboard Overview Screenshot]

The dashboard displays a live overview of your visitor engagement data, automatically updating as visitors interact with your chat widget.

## Step 3: Choose your reporting timeframe

In the top right corner of the dashboard, click the reporting period dropdown button to choose your preferred date range:

- **Last 7 days:** Best for observing day-to-day fluctuations, weekly traffic patterns, and immediate results from new promotions.
- **Last 30 days:** Ideal for monthly reporting and reviewing long-term visitor trends.
- **Last 6 months:** Useful for tracking overall seasonal growth and evaluating communication channel adoption over time.

[Add Analytics Period Dropdown Screenshot]

Selecting a timeframe instantly updates the KPI summary cards, the bar chart, and the channel breakdown table below.

## Step 4: Review your primary KPI summary cards

Three summary cards give you a quick health check of your customer engagement:

1. **Views:** Displays total impressions, which is the number of times your floating chat launcher appeared on screen for unique visitor sessions.
2. **Clicks:** Displays total channel interactions, recording every time a visitor clicked your launcher, expanded a channel menu, or tapped a direct communication button.
3. **Conv. Rate:** Displays your click-through conversion rate, calculated as total clicks divided by total views. A higher rate indicates that visitors find your chat prompt appealing and relevant.

[Add KPI Summary Cards Screenshot]

## Step 5: Explore the daily clicks volume chart

Below the KPI cards, the interactive bar chart titled **Clicks over the last 7 days** (or your selected timeframe) illustrates daily interaction volume:

[Add Interactive Clicks Bar Chart Screenshot]

- Each bar represents a day within the reporting period, with height corresponding to total click volume.
- Hover your mouse over any bar to view an instant tooltip showing the exact click count for that specific day.
- The highest-performing day is automatically highlighted with a vibrant accent color so you can spot peak traffic periods at a glance.

## Step 6: Analyze the channel performance breakdown

Scroll down to the **Channel Performance Breakdown** section to see which messaging apps your audience prefers.

[Add Channel Breakdown Table Screenshot]

This table lists every active channel alongside:

- The platform brand icon and label.
- Total click count recorded during the selected period.
- A proportional color progress bar showing that channel percentage share of total clicks.
- The percentage share number, making it easy to identify your most valuable customer service channels.

## Check that it works

To verify that your analytics tracking is active:

1. Open your published website in an incognito or private browser window.
2. Scroll until the floating chat launcher appears, then click on the button and click on one of your channel links.
3. Reopen the Chat Bubbles plugin in Framer, navigate to the **Analytics** tab, and confirm that your view and click counts have increased.

## Troubleshooting

- **The Analytics tab does not appear in the top navigation bar:** Go to **Settings** and ensure the **Enable Analytics** toggle is turned on, because the plugin hides the tab when tracking is disabled to keep your workspace focused.
- **Views increase but clicks remain at zero:** Check that your channel handles and numbers are properly configured in the **Builder** tab so that clicking a button initiates a valid conversation.
- **You want to reset analytics counts:** Contact Framefic support or disconnect and reinstall the component if you need to wipe historic test data before launching an official marketing campaign.
