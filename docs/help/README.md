# Chat Bubbles Help Documentation

Welcome to the user documentation for Chat Bubbles, the multi-channel live chat and social contact button plugin for Framer. These guides will help you set up your chat buttons, introduce your customer support team, customize your colors and animations, and track your conversion results.

---

## Terms used in these docs

To keep these guides clear and consistent, the same words are used throughout every page:

- **Floating launcher:** The circular, rounded, or pill button that stays anchored to a corner of your website so visitors can open your chat options at any time.
- **Live Chat Modal:** The popup chat window that displays your header gradient, support team members, greeting speech bubble, and conversation start button.
- **Icon Buttons:** The classic menu mode that expands your active messaging platforms into a vertical stack, horizontal row, or two-column grid.
- **Channel:** Any of the 19 supported communication services, such as WhatsApp, Messenger, Telegram, Phone, SMS, or Email.
- **Agent:** An individual team member shown in your live chat modal with a personal avatar photo, department role, online status dot, and assigned direct contact channel.
- **Greeting bubble:** The small floating message preview that appears beside your launcher button after a short delay to invite visitors into a conversation.
- **Smart trigger:** An automated condition, such as visitor scroll depth or mouse exit intent, that displays your chat widget at the most effective moment.
- **Operating schedule:** Your configured business hours and active days of the week, which automatically manage online badges and offline notices.
- **Canvas component:** The native Framer code component that Chat Bubbles generates and places directly onto your project canvas.
