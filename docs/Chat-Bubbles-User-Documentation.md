## Terms Used in These Docs

To keep these guides clear and consistent, the same words are used throughout every page:

- **Floating launcher:** The circular, rounded, or pill button that stays anchored to a corner of your website so visitors can open your chat options at any time.
- **Live Chat Modal:** The popup chat window that displays your header gradient, support team members, greeting speech bubble, and conversation start button.
- **Icon Buttons:** The classic menu mode that expands your active messaging platforms into a vertical stack, horizontal row, or two-column grid.
- **Channel:** Any of the 19 supported communication services, such as WhatsApp, Messenger, Telegram, Phone, SMS, or Email.
- **Agent:** An individual team member shown in your live chat modal with a personal avatar photo, department role, online status dot, and assigned direct contact channel.
- **Greeting bubble:** The small floating message preview that appears beside your launcher button after a short delay to invite visitors into a conversation.
- **Smart trigger:** An automated condition, such as visitor scroll depth or mouse exit intent, that displays your chat widget at the most effective moment.
- **Operating schedule:** Your configured business hours and active days of the week, which automatically manage online badges and offline notices.
- **Canvas component:** The native Framer code component that Chat Bubbles generates and places directly onto your project canvas.

# How to get started with Chat Bubbles

This guide walks you through setting up your first floating chat button in Framer, connecting your favorite messaging apps, and publishing the widget to your live website in just a few minutes.

## Before you start

Make sure you have an active project open in your Framer desktop app or web browser, because you will insert your chat component directly onto your project canvas.

## Video tutorial

[Add Getting Started Video Tutorial]

## Step 1: Open the Chat Bubbles plugin in Framer

Open your Framer project, click the **Plugins** icon in the top toolbar or press `Shift + I`, and search for **Chat Bubbles**. Click the plugin name to launch the configuration window.

[Add Plugin Launch Window Screenshot]

When the window opens, you will see the **Home** tab with an overview card showing your active widget mode, your active channel count, and your support team count.

## Step 2: Choose your widget experience mode

Click the **Design** tab in the top navigation bar and look under **Widget Experience Mode** to decide how your visitors will interact when clicking your floating button:

- Select **Live Chat Modal** if you want a modern chat popup with your team member avatars, a custom header gradient, and an introductory greeting bubble.
- Select **Icon Buttons** if you prefer a classic floating menu that expands directly into a list of social platform buttons.

[Add Widget Experience Mode Selection Screenshot]

Clicking either card updates your widget mode immediately so you can preview the layout as you work.

## Step 3: Enter your primary messaging details

Click the **Builder** tab in the top navigation bar, then make sure the **Channels** sub-tab is selected. You will see a list of 19 supported communication platforms with **WhatsApp**, **Messenger**, and **Telegram** enabled by default.

[Add Channels Tab Overview Screenshot]

Find your preferred platform and enter your contact information:

1. For **WhatsApp**, enter your phone number with your international country code in the input field, such as `+1 234 567 8900`.
2. Expand the card to add a **Pre-filled Welcome Message** if you want your visitors to start conversations with a ready-made greeting.
3. Use the toggle switch on the right side of any channel card to enable or disable that platform.

[Add WhatsApp Channel Configuration Screenshot]

## Step 4: Insert the component onto your Framer canvas

Once your contact details are in place, scroll to the bottom of the plugin window and click the **Insert into Canvas** button.

[Add Insert into Canvas Button Screenshot]

The plugin will generate a clean Framer component and place it on your active canvas. A notification banner will confirm that your component has been created. You can drag the component into your main layout frame or leave it anchored to your page.

## Step 5: Publish your site and test the live chat

Click the **Publish** button in the top right corner of your Framer interface, then open your live website URL in a new browser tab.

[Add Published Site Preview Screenshot]

You will see your floating chat button anchored cleanly to the bottom right corner of your screen. Click the button to confirm that your messaging modal opens and that clicking your channel launches the correct conversation.

## Check that it works

To verify that your setup is working properly:

1. Visit your published site on both desktop and mobile devices.
2. Click the floating chat button to open the modal or button menu.
3. Click your **WhatsApp** or social channel button. Your browser should open the corresponding chat app with your phone number or profile pre-loaded.
4. If you have analytics enabled in **Settings**, reopen the plugin after testing and click the **Analytics** tab to see your test clicks recorded in real time.

## Troubleshooting

- **The button does not appear after publishing:** Check your Framer layers panel to ensure the Chat Bubbles component is inside your primary page frame rather than positioned off-canvas.
- **WhatsApp shows an invalid phone number error:** Verify that your phone number includes your international country code without any special symbols, brackets, or leading zeros.
- **You want to change your colors or button position:** Reopen the plugin, adjust your settings in the **Design** or **Settings** tabs, and click **Update Component** to refresh your canvas instance without replacing it.

# How to configure messaging channels

This guide helps you connect, customize, and organize your communication channels so your visitors can reach you through the messaging apps they already use and trust.

## Before you start

Gather your official social handles, customer support phone numbers, email addresses, and community invite links before you begin, because having these on hand makes entering your channel details quick and effortless.

## Video tutorial

[Add Configuring Messaging Channels Video Tutorial]

## Step 1: Open the Channels builder

Click the **Builder** tab in the top navigation bar, then select the **Channels** sub-tab. You will see a list of 19 supported platforms with their official brand icons, descriptive hints, and status toggles.

[Add Channels List Overview Screenshot]

Channels that are already enabled display an **Active** pill next to their title. You can click the arrow icon on the right side of any active card to expand or minimize its configuration options.

## Step 2: Enable your preferred platforms

Browse the list of supported channels and use the toggle switch on the right side of any platform to turn it on:

- **WhatsApp:** Direct chat via web or mobile app.
- **Messenger:** Direct chat through Facebook page or username.
- **Instagram:** Direct profile messaging.
- **Telegram:** Instant messaging via username or channel.
- **TikTok:** Profile connection for social engagement.
- **WeChat:** WeChat ID or QR code profile link.
- **Viber:** Direct messaging via verified phone number.
- **LINE:** Official or personal LINE account connection.
- **Signal:** Secure communication via profile handle or link.
- **Phone:** Direct phone dialer link for mobile and desktop callers.
- **SMS:** Mobile text messaging with ready-to-send messages.
- **Email:** Direct email client link with subject and body pre-filled.
- **Discord:** Community or server invite link.
- **Slack:** Team workspace or community channel invite URL.
- **Microsoft Teams:** Direct chat email or meeting link.
- **X (Twitter):** Profile link or direct message handle.
- **LinkedIn:** Personal profile or company page link.
- **Google Maps:** Direct link to your physical storefront or office location.
- **Custom URL Link:** Any external web address, booking page, or custom link protocol.

[Add Enable Channel Toggle Screenshot]

## Step 3: Enter your contact handle or number

In each enabled channel card, enter your handle or number in the primary input field. The placeholder text gives you a clear example of the expected format:

- For phone-based channels like **WhatsApp**, **Viber**, **Phone**, and **SMS**, enter your complete phone number including your country code, such as `+1 234 567 8900`.
- For handle-based platforms like **Instagram**, **Telegram**, and **TikTok**, enter your username without the `@` symbol.
- For **Messenger**, enter your public Facebook page name or profile username.

[Add Channel Handle Input Screenshot]

## Step 4: Customize your channel button colors

Every platform comes with its official brand color by default, but you can change any button color to match your website theme. Click the color circle in the **Button Color** field to open the color picker.

[Add Custom Color Picker Screenshot]

You can choose from the curated color palette, enter a custom HEX code, or use the interactive color gradient slider to find your ideal shade.

## Step 5: Add pre-filled messages and email subjects

For **WhatsApp**, **Telegram**, and **SMS**, you can fill in the **Pre-filled Welcome Message** field so that when visitors click the button, their chat window opens with a draft message already written for them.

[Add Pre-filled Welcome Message Screenshot]

You can make your messages dynamic by using two automatic placeholder tags:

- `{title}`: Inserts the title of the page the visitor is currently viewing.
- `{url}`: Inserts the full web address of the current page.

For example, typing `Hello! I have a question about {title} at {url}.` will automatically substitute your product or article name when the visitor clicks the button.

If you enable **Email**, you can enter a default **Email Subject** such as `Inquiry from website`, and write a template in the **Pre-filled Email Message Body** field so inquiries reach your inbox with structured context.

[Add Email Channel Configuration Screenshot]

## Step 6: Create custom links for external tools

If you use a third-party booking calendar, a knowledge base, or a custom web app, enable the **Custom URL Link** platform at the bottom of the list.

[Add Custom Channel Configuration Screenshot]

When configuring a custom link:

1. Enter your destination address in the **Custom URL Link** field, such as `https://calendly.com/your-team/30min`.
2. Enter a friendly name in the **Channel / Button Label** field, such as `Book a Call` or `Help Docs`.
3. Pick a custom **Button Color** to distinguish it from standard chat icons.
4. Use the **Open Link In** toggle to choose whether the link opens in a **New browser tab** or stays in the **Same browser tab**.

## Check that it works

After configuring your channels, click **Update Component** or **Insert into Canvas** in the bottom action bar. Publish your Framer site and test your buttons:

1. Click your floating chat button to open your channel list or modal.
2. Click each enabled channel to verify that it directs visitors to the correct profile, phone dialer, or messaging app.
3. Check that your pre-filled welcome text appears correctly in WhatsApp and that the `{title}` tag displays your page title.

## Troubleshooting

- **WhatsApp opens a blank chat without your pre-filled message:** Ensure you entered text into the **Pre-filled Welcome Message** field and updated your canvas component before publishing.
- **Your custom link does not open when clicked:** Check that your link begins with `https://` or `http://` so the browser recognizes it as a valid web destination.
- **You enabled a channel but it does not appear in the floating menu:** Verify that the channel has both its toggle turned on and a non-empty handle or URL entered into its primary field.

# How to add support team agents

This guide explains how to build a welcoming team roster inside your live chat modal so visitors can choose the right person or department for their inquiry.

## Before you start

Make sure your widget experience mode is set to **Live Chat Modal** on the **Design** tab, because team agents appear specifically inside the interactive modal window. Also ensure you have enabled at least one channel on the **Channels** tab so your agents have platforms to receive messages.

## Video tutorial

[Add Adding Support Team Agents Video Tutorial]

## Step 1: Open the Support Team builder

Click the **Builder** tab in the top navigation bar, then click the **Support Team** sub-tab. You will see your active team roster with preset agents, along with their avatar photos, names, department roles, and assigned communication channels.

[Add Support Team Overview Screenshot]

Each agent card includes a gear icon to configure detailed settings, and a remove icon to delete the agent if needed.

## Step 2: Add a new team member

Click the **Add Team Member** button at the bottom of the list to open the new agent form.

[Add Add Team Member Form Screenshot]

Enter the basic details for your team member:

1. **Agent Name:** Enter the full name or display name of the specialist, such as `Emma Watson`.
2. **Role / Department:** Enter their job title or area of expertise, such as `Sales Specialist` or `Billing Support`.
3. **Assigned Channel:** Pick from your enabled channels in the dropdown menu so incoming messages route to the right platform.

## Step 3: Choose or upload an avatar photo

You can provide a friendly face for every agent using any of three avatar options in the **Avatar Photo** section:

- **Upload Image:** Click **Choose Image File** to upload a JPG, PNG, WebP, or SVG file from your computer. The photo uploads securely to the Framer content delivery network for fast global loading.
- **Presets:** Click the **Presets** tab and select one of ten professionally illustrated avatar icons for a polished look without taking your own photos.
- **Image URL:** Click the **Image URL** tab and paste a public image link if your team photos are already hosted online.

[Add Avatar Selection Options Screenshot]

Click **Add to Team** when finished to insert your new agent into the roster.

## Step 4: Configure direct numbers and custom greetings

To customize an agent's individual contact details, click the gear icon on their card to expand their advanced settings.

[Add Agent Advanced Settings Screenshot]

You can configure several personalized options:

- **Direct Channel Number or Handle:** If an agent has their own phone extension, WhatsApp number, or Telegram username, enter it here. Leaving this field blank means the agent will inherit the default number configured in the **Channels** tab.
- **Direct Pre-filled Message:** Write a personal greeting that pre-fills when visitors choose this specific agent, such as `Hi there! Emma here from billing. How can I help you today?`.
- **Direct Email Subject:** If the agent is assigned to email, you can customize the subject line for inquiries directed to them.

## Step 5: Set online and offline availability

Every agent card features an **Active / Online** toggle switch.

[Add Agent Online Status Toggle Screenshot]

- When toggled on, a vibrant green status dot appears on the agent avatar in your live chat modal, signaling to visitors that this team member is available to respond quickly.
- When toggled off, the status dot switches to an away indicator, letting visitors know the agent is currently offline.

Click **Done** at the bottom of the expanded card to collapse the settings once your changes are complete.

## Check that it works

After configuring your team, click **Update Component** in the bottom action bar, publish your Framer site, and open your live page:

1. Click the floating launcher button to open your live chat modal.
2. Verify that all team members appear in the roster with their correct avatars, names, roles, and status indicators.
3. Click an agent to verify that the conversation opens directly to their assigned channel and includes their custom greeting text.

## Troubleshooting

- **A channel does not appear in the Assigned Channel dropdown:** Make sure the channel is enabled on the **Channels** sub-tab first, because only active platforms appear in the team assignment menu.
- **An uploaded avatar does not display properly:** Verify that your image file is a valid JPG, PNG, or WebP format under 5 megabytes in size so that Framer can process the upload cleanly.
- **Changes to agent names do not appear on your published site:** Remember to click **Update Component** in the bottom bar and re-publish your Framer project to push your latest edits live.

# How to customize widget design

This guide walks you through styling every visual element of your chat widget, including the floating launcher button, menu layout, header gradients, entrance animations, and responsive mobile spacing.

## Before you start

Review your website color palette and typography so you can match your chat widget seamlessly to your existing design language.

## Video tutorial

[Add Customizing Widget Design Video Tutorial]

## Step 1: Select your widget mode

On the **Design** tab, review the **Widget Experience Mode** section:

- Choose **Live Chat Modal** to display an interactive popup window containing your header gradient, team member roster, speech bubble preview, and start chat action button.
- Choose **Icon Buttons** to expand your active channels as a floating icon list without opening a larger window.

[Add Widget Mode Switcher Screenshot]

Your selection here determines which design options appear in the **Design** tab.

## Step 2: Customize the modal header and theme gradient

Click the **Design** tab in the top navigation bar. Look under **Chat Window Header & Theme** to style your modal:

1. **Header Theme Gradient:** Select one of the curated brand gradients, such as **WhatsApp**, **Messenger**, **Telegram**, **Instagram**, **TikTok**, **WeChat**, or **Modern Dark**.
2. **Custom Gradient:** Select **Custom** to open the interactive gradient builder. You can pick your starting color, ending color, and rotation angle, or select from curated gradient swatches.
3. **Header Title:** Enter your main greeting, such as `Hi there!`.
4. **Response Time Badge:** Enter an encouraging turnaround time, such as `Typically replies in minutes` or `Online now`.
5. **Header Subtitle:** Enter a welcoming prompt that invites visitors to ask questions.

[Add Header Theme Gradient Screenshot]

## Step 3: Style the chat preview and call to action

Scroll down to the **Chat Preview & Start CTA** section:

- **Welcome Chat Bubble Message:** Enter the friendly message that appears inside the simulated chat bubble in your modal window, such as `Hello! How can someone assist today?`.
- **CTA Button Text:** Enter the label for the primary action button at the bottom of the modal, such as `Start chat` or `Send a message`.

[Add Chat Preview and CTA Settings Screenshot]

## Step 4: Configure the floating launcher button

In the **Floating Launcher Design** section, tailor the appearance of the button that sits permanently on your visitor screen:

1. **Toggle Button Icon:** Choose the icon that represents your business best, such as **Live Chat (Speech Bubble)**, **WhatsApp**, **Messenger**, **Telegram**, **Instagram**, **Phone Call**, **Email**, or **Customer Support (Headset)**.
2. **Button Shape:** Choose **Circle**, **Rounded Square**, or **Pill** to match the border radii of other buttons on your site.
3. **Button Size:** Choose **Small (46px)** for compact pages, **Medium (56px)** for standard layouts, or **Large (64px)** for high visibility.
4. **Launcher Default Color:** Select your brand accent color using the custom color picker.

[Add Floating Launcher Shape and Icon Screenshot]

## Step 5: Choose menu layout and entrance animations

If you use the **Icon Buttons** mode or want to adjust transition effects:

- **Button Menu Layout:** Choose **Stack (Vertical)** to display channels in an upright column, **Grid (2 Columns)** to save vertical space, or **Horizontal Row** to stretch buttons across the bottom.
- **Entrance Animation:** Select **Pop In** for a spring effect, **Playful Bounce** for a lively arrival, **Slide Up** for a smooth upward reveal, **Pulse Glow** for an eye-catching heartbeat, or **None (Instant)** for an immediate appearance.

[Add Menu Layout and Animation Settings Screenshot]

## Step 6: Adjust desktop and mobile corner offsets

Control the exact pixel distance between your floating button and the edges of the browser window:

1. **Desktop Offset X (px)** and **Desktop Offset Y (px):** Set horizontal and vertical margins for desktop screens, with default values of 24 pixels each.
2. **Mobile Offset X (px)** and **Mobile Offset Y (px):** Set separate margins for mobile screens under 640 pixels wide, with default values of 16 pixels each.
3. **Mobile Layout Constraint (Cap 4+):** Keep this toggle enabled if you have five or more channels active. It automatically limits visible mobile buttons to your top four channels and adds an expandable `+ More` button, keeping your mobile layout clean and uncluttered.

[Add Mobile Spacing and Cap Settings Screenshot]

## Step 7: Configure unread badges and accessibility labels

Add finishing touches for visitor engagement and accessibility:

# How to configure page targeting rules

This guide walks you through controlling where your chat widget appears across your website, from showing it on every page to targeting specific product sections or hiding it on checkout flows.

## Before you start

List the pages on your site where you want visitors to see chat assistance, such as high-intent pricing or support pages, and note any pages where you prefer an uncluttered view, such as minimal landing pages.

[Add Page Targeting Rules Video Tutorial]

## Step 1: Open Page Targeting in Settings

Click the **Settings** tab in the top navigation bar, then scroll down to the fourth card under **Behavioral Triggers & Smart Display** titled **Page / URL Targeting Filter**.

[Add Page Targeting Filter Card Screenshot]

This card controls the visibility logic that runs in the visitor browser when each page loads.

## Step 2: Choose your display target

Click the dropdown menu labeled **Where the widget will be shown?**:

- Select **Every page** if you want your floating chat button to remain accessible across your entire site. This is the default setting for most websites.
- Select a specific Framer page name, such as `Home`, `Pricing`, or `Contact`, if you only want the chat widget to appear on that single page.
- Select **Custom URL Paths & Wildcards...** if you want advanced control over multiple URL paths and subdirectories.

[Add Where Widget Will Be Shown Dropdown Screenshot]

## Step 3: Select your custom targeting mode

When you select **Custom URL Paths & Wildcards...**, two additional configuration fields will appear:

[Add Custom Targeting Mode Fields Screenshot]

In the **Targeting Mode** dropdown, choose your filtering approach:

- Select **Show Widget Only on Listed Paths** to hide the widget by default and only display it on the specific URL paths you enter. This approach is helpful when you only want chat on high-intent sales pages.
- Select **Hide Widget on Listed Paths (Show Everywhere Else)** to keep the widget visible across your entire website except on the specific pages you list. This approach is ideal for hiding chat on checkout, thank you, or distraction-free webinar pages.

## Step 4: Enter your URL paths and wildcards

In the **URL Paths & Wildcards** textarea, enter the paths you want to target, separating each entry onto a new line or with a comma:

- **Exact path matching:** Enter the precise path starting with a forward slash, such as `/pricing` or `/checkout`.
- **Wildcard matching:** Add an asterisk symbol (`*`) to target an entire section or folder structure:
  - `/blog/*` targets every post inside your blog directory, such as `/blog/news` and `/blog/announcements`.
  - `/products/*` targets all product detail pages in your store.
  - `/account/*` targets all customer account sub-pages.

[Add URL Paths Textarea Screenshot]

For example, to display chat only on your pricing page and product catalog, select **Show Widget Only on Listed Paths** and type:

```text
/pricing
/products/*
```

2. Visit a URL that you excluded or left out of an inclusion list. Confirm that the widget remains completely hidden without leaving any blank placeholder frames.
3. Check browser console logs if needed to verify that the targeting evaluation executes without errors.

## Troubleshooting

- **The widget shows on pages you intended to exclude:** Ensure each path in your list begins with a leading forward slash, such as `/checkout` rather than `checkout`.
- **Wildcards do not match child pages:** Check that your wildcard entry ends with `/*` rather than just `*`, for example `/blog/*` instead of `/blog*`.
- **You selected a single Framer page from the dropdown but renamed it later:** If you rename a page inside Framer, reopen the plugin, select the updated page name from the **Where the widget will be shown?** dropdown, and click **Update Component**.

## Frequently asked questions

### 1. How should I format phone numbers for WhatsApp, Viber, and SMS?

Always enter phone numbers in international format, including your country code:

- For United States and Canada: `+1 234 567 8900`
- For United Kingdom: `+44 7911 123456`
- For Australia: `+61 412 345 678`
- For Germany: `+49 151 12345678`

Do not include leading zeros, dashes, or parentheses in the number field, because messaging services like WhatsApp Web use raw international digits to initiate chats.

### 2. Why does the notification sound chime not play automatically on mobile phones?

Modern mobile operating systems, including iOS and Android, have strict browser autoplay policies designed to prevent unwanted audio playback. Mobile browsers require a visitor to interact with the page first, such as tapping or scrolling, before allowing web applications to produce sound.

Once your visitor begins interacting with your page, the synthesized greeting chime will play normally when your appearance delay timer triggers.

---

### 3. How does Chat Bubbles work across Framer responsive breakpoints?

Chat Bubbles is built to adapt automatically across Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile layouts:

- On desktop screens, your full channel list or live chat modal appears using your configured **Desktop Offset X (px)** and **Desktop Offset Y (px)** margins.
- On screens under 640 pixels wide, the widget applies your **Mobile Offset X (px)** and **Mobile Offset Y (px)** settings.
- If you have five or more channels active, the **Mobile Layout Constraint (Cap 4+)** feature automatically caps visible mobile buttons to your top four platforms and adds a clean `+ More` expand button, keeping your mobile screen tidy.

[Add Responsive Breakpoint Preview Screenshot]

---

### 4. What should I do if the Framer canvas displays a duplicate component warning?

If you see a notice on your canvas stating that a duplicate component has been detected, check your Framer layers list. You only need to place one Chat Bubbles component on your primary page frame:

1. Select your page frame in the layers panel.
2. Verify that you have only one instance of the Chat Bubbles component inserted.
3. If you have multiple breakpoint frames, such as separate Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile variants on the same page, keep the component within your primary shared frame so it inherits seamlessly across all screen sizes.

---

### 5. Why do my latest changes in the plugin not show on my live website?

Whenever you adjust settings in the plugin interface, remember to click the **Update Component** button located in the bottom action bar.

[Add Update Component Button Screenshot]

After the green confirmation banner appears, click the **Publish** button in the top right corner of Framer to build and push your new production bundle live.

---

### 6. Can I position the floating button in a different corner?

Yes. Open the **Settings** tab and look under **Widget Placement**. Click the **Screen Corner Position** dropdown to choose from four options:

- **Bottom Right (Recommended)**
- **Bottom Left**
- **Top Right**
- **Top Left**

[Add Screen Corner Position Dropdown Screenshot]

Selecting a different corner moves both the floating launcher and any opened menus or modals to that designated area.

---

### 7. Does the analytics tracker store personal visitor data or use tracking cookies?

No. The built-in analytics engine is designed for privacy. It counts anonymous page impressions and button clicks using lightweight aggregation, without storing personal visitor data, IP addresses, or tracking cookies. You can safely run Chat Bubbles on privacy-conscious websites without requiring cookie consent banners for the chat widget.

---

### 8. How do I transfer my PRO license to another Framer project?

Each Chat Bubbles license is attached to a specific Framer site ID. If you start a new Framer project and want to transfer your existing subscription:
